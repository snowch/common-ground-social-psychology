SYNTHESIZED NARRATION

Narration was generated from the course scripts using Kokoro-82M v1.0,
voice af_heart, speed 1.0, through kokoro-onnx. The recordings, model and
voice-file hashes, and production workflow are documented in production/.

Kokoro model: https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
Kokoro implementation: https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro
Upstream model metadata declares Apache-2.0; a copy of the Apache-2.0
license is retained in licenses/Kokoro-Apache-2.0.txt.
kokoro-onnx: https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx
MIT license retained in licenses/kokoro-onnx-MIT.txt.

The course distributes synthesized recordings, not the model weights,
voice tensors, or upstream speech software. Those software/model licenses
are recorded for provenance; this notice does not apply them wholesale
