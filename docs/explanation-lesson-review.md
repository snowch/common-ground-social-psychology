# Independent lesson: test the explanation

The old evidence screen combined explaining attribution patterns with a procedure for testing an explanation. The procedure was hidden as an alternative visualization, although it teaches a separate skill. Learners now encounter it as a normal, independently narrated lesson: “Test the explanation.”

Learning design follows the repository’s credited [Instructional Design skill](https://github.com/joelrkohlman/instructional-design/blob/main/SKILL.md). The performance is to describe an observed action, compare occasions, test alternatives, and revise confidence. Four concise teaching points sit on the slide. Narration works through Maya’s late arrival and explicitly distinguishes reviewing Leo’s reasoning from testing the learner’s own interpretation. The earlier lesson focuses on attribution and the hypothetical actor–observer comparison. Both lessons are paced by the learner’s Next action and have separate Play narration controls.

Visual design follows the credited [Frontend Design skill](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md). Retain Common Ground’s pale blue paper, dark ink, ocean accent, plum inference, brass rules, Libre Baskerville headings and Arimo text. Replace the large generic flowchart boxes with a small claim panel and a left-aligned numbered sequence, with concrete prompts about Maya. The line connects a genuine sequence. The final filled marker emphasizes revising confidence. Tablet controls remain between the text and the diagram; desktop uses the existing two-column layout.

Chapter 1 now has nine steps; other chapters retain eight. There are 81 slides and 151 audio clips. The new lesson uses a stable identity, c01s05b, inserted after c01s05. Existing slide identities and bookmark URLs are preserved. Navigation, chapter totals, progress, resume state, print page ranges, and hosted/offline recording counts now derive from chapter content. Old saved positions migrate by their former slide identity. Opening a standalone chapter retains progress from other chapters.

The repeated-evidence figure is the new lesson’s primary illustration and is removed from the preceding lesson’s optional figures. The visual companion still contains 48 figures, now grouped under their correct lesson. Chapter 1’s PDF has nine pages; the complete deck has 81.

Validation covers the new lesson’s independent audio, forward/back navigation, chapter boundaries, existing bookmarks, old resume state, saved answers, cross-chapter progress retention, and standalone chapter. All 81 screens are checked at five widths. PDF export, essential vocabulary, visual bounds and accessibility checks cover the changed placement. Both changed recordings use the pinned Kokoro ONNX model, af_heart voice, speed 1.0, and Whisper small.en screening. Automated checks do not constitute a learner pilot or human listening approval.
