First private release of **Common Ground**, an original introductory social psychology course in ten stories.

- 80 illustrated slides across ten chapters.
- Recorded Kokoro `af_heart` narration at speed 1.0, including quiz feedback and recall answers.
- 20 application decisions, 20 recall questions, and ten field exercises.
- Separate self-contained chapter decks, PDFs, and approximately 38 minutes of chapter audio.
- Complete narration scripts, production provenance, source links, and automated review records.

Download `common-ground-af-heart.zip`, extract it, and open `common-ground/index.html` in a browser. Press **Play narration**. No installed device voice or speech service is required. The full ZIP is approximately 137 MiB. `SHA256SUMS.txt` verifies the downloaded archive.

The repository remains private, and release downloads inherit its private access. The repository contains the editable source and build instructions; the ZIP is the completed portable viewing package. Build outputs and the ZIP are kept out of Git history.

The audio was screened independently with Whisper and inspected as phonemes; playback and interactions were tested in Chromium. This is not a human listening review or a claim of full textbook equivalence. The course is independently written and does not reproduce Aronson textbook passages, images, or exercises.
